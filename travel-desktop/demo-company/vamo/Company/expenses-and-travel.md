# Vamo — Expenses and work travel

Version: 1. Owner: Noah Berg, Operations lead. Scope: all employees. Fictional demo policy, 29 October 2026.

Work travel: Agree the dates, purpose and budget with your lead before booking a city scouting trip. Wait for approval before paying.
Expenses: Keep an itemised receipt, record the business purpose, and submit the monthly expense report to Operations by the last working day of the month.
Missing receipt: Ask Operations how to document the expense. Do not invent or recreate a receipt.
