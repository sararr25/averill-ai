# Vamo — Handover and escalation

Version: 1. Owner: Noah Berg, Operations lead. Scope: all employees. Fictional demo policy, 29 October 2026.

Handover: Write the current status, next action, responsible person, due date and source links. Confirm that the receiving colleague has accepted ownership.
Urgent issue: Tell your lead immediately if an issue affects a customer departure today. Operations coordinates the response; do not promise a refund or supplier change without approval.
Policy question: Ask People about leave and onboarding, Operations about trips and expenses, and Marketing about publication and brand requirements. If approved documents conflict, ask the responsible lead which version applies.
