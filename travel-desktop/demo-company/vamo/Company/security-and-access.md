# Vamo — Security and access

Version: 1. Owner: Alex Holm, company owner. Scope: all employees. Fictional demo policy, 29 October 2026.

Account access: Request only the access you need through your lead. Never share passwords, sign-in codes or API keys in a document, chat or design.
Customer information: Use the approved company storage and share customer information only with colleagues who need it. Do not paste customer records into public searches or AI tools.
Security incident: If a device or account may be compromised, stop using it for customer work and contact your lead and Operations immediately.
