# Vamo — Remote work and team meetings

Version: 1. Owner: Lina Sørensen, People lead. Scope: all employees. Fictional demo policy, 29 October 2026.

Remote work: Agree remote working days with your lead, show your availability in the team calendar, and keep customer handovers covered. Ask People before working from another country.
Team meeting: The all-company weekly planning meeting is Monday at 09:30 Europe/Copenhagen. Bring your priorities, blockers and decisions needed.
Meeting notes: Record the decision, owner and next action in the shared handover after each planning meeting. Keep personal or restricted information out of shared notes.
