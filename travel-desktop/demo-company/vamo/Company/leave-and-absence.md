# Vamo — Leave and absence

Version: 1. Owner: Lina Sørensen, People lead. Scope: all employees. Fictional demo policy, 29 October 2026. This is a demonstration workflow, not employment-law advice.

Annual leave: Request holidays at least two weeks in advance in the shared team calendar and ask your lead to approve the dates. Add your handover owner before your leave starts. A calendar entry alone is not approval.
Sick leave: Tell your lead before your normal start time, mark yourself unavailable in the team calendar, and contact People if you need help. Do not put medical details in shared calendar entries.
Leave changes: Ask your lead before changing approved leave dates. People keeps the final absence record.
