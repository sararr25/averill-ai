# Vamo — Supplier checklist

Version: 1. Owner: Noah Berg, Operations lead. Synthetic demo checklist, 1 October 2026.

Before a trip is confirmed, Operations checks each supplier for the traveller's dates: hostel or hotel availability, cancellation conditions, group size limits and the final price in DKK. Record who checked and when. Anything not yet checked stays marked as awaiting confirmation in the trip brief.

Suppliers in this demo are fictional; no supplier system is connected.
