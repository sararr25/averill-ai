# Vamo — Social media guidelines

Version: 2. Owner: Maya Jensen, Marketing lead. Synthetic demo guide, 10 October 2026.

Post on Instagram twice a week and on LinkedIn twice a week during the Winter Escapes 2027 campaign. Instagram captions are lowercase and playful; LinkedIn posts use sentence case and speak about how Vamo works.

Credit every photographer in the caption. Reply to comments within one working day, and hand any booking or price question to Operations.

## Hashtags
Every Instagram and LinkedIn post carries the brand tag #vamotravel. Instagram posts use up to 8 hashtags and LinkedIn posts use up to 3. Do not use misleading or competitor-style tags such as #cheapestflights, #lowestprice or #bestprice, because Vamo has not approved a price guarantee. A paid Instagram Reel must also carry #ad. This adds to the "Paid partnership with Vamo" caption text and the platform partnership label, and does not replace them.

Hashtag rule: instagram | required | "#vamotravel".
Hashtag rule: linkedin | required | "#vamotravel".
Hashtag rule: instagram | max | "8".
Hashtag rule: linkedin | max | "3".
Hashtag rule: any | banned | "#cheapestflights".
Hashtag rule: any | banned | "#lowestprice".
Hashtag rule: any | banned | "#bestprice".
Hashtag rule: instagram | paid-required | "#ad".
