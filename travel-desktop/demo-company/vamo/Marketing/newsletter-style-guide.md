# Vamo — Newsletter style guide

Version: 1. Owner: Maya Jensen, Marketing lead. Synthetic demo guide, 30 September 2026.

## Voice
Write like a friend who has just come back from the city: warm, direct and specific. Open with "Hey traveller," and sign off as "The Vamo crew" or with the sender's first name.

## Structure
One idea per email. Keep the body under 200 words, use a short numbered or ticked list for tips, and end with one question readers can answer by replying.

## Subject lines
Under 45 characters, sentence case, at most one emoji. Describe what is inside; avoid urgency or scarcity wording.

## Sending
Newsletters go out on Wednesdays at 10:00 Copenhagen time, at most twice a month. The welcome series is automated and is edited only by the Marketing lead.
