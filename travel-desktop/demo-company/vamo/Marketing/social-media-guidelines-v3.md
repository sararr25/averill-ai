# Vamo — Social media guidelines

Version: 3. Owner: Maya Jensen, Marketing lead. Synthetic demo guide, 28 October 2026.

Post on Instagram twice a week and on LinkedIn twice a week during the Winter Escapes 2027 campaign. Instagram captions are lowercase and playful; LinkedIn posts use sentence case and speak about how Vamo works.

Credit every photographer in the caption. Reply to comments within one working day, and hand any booking or price question to Operations.

## Hashtags
Every Instagram and LinkedIn post carries the brand tag #vamotravel. Instagram posts use up to 8 hashtags and LinkedIn posts use up to 3. Do not use misleading or competitor-style tags such as #cheapestflights, #lowestprice or #bestprice, because Vamo has not approved a price guarantee. A paid Instagram Reel must also carry #ad. This adds to the "Paid partnership with Vamo" caption text and the platform partnership label, and does not replace them.

Hashtag rule: instagram | required | "#vamotravel".
Hashtag rule: linkedin | required | "#vamotravel".
Hashtag rule: instagram | max | "8".
Hashtag rule: linkedin | max | "3".
Hashtag rule: any | banned | "#cheapestflights".
Hashtag rule: any | banned | "#lowestprice".
Hashtag rule: any | banned | "#bestprice".
Hashtag rule: instagram | paid-required | "#ad".

## Images
Every Instagram and LinkedIn post carries an image. Instagram feed images use a ratio from 4:5 to 1.91:1, which includes 1:1, and Reels and Stories use 9:16. LinkedIn images use 1.91:1, 1:1 or 4:5. Instagram images are at least 1080 pixels wide. Every image has alt text that describes it. Text printed on an image follows the same claim rules as the caption, so an image must not say "lowest prices guaranteed" either.

Image rule: instagram | required | "image".
Image rule: linkedin | required | "image".
Image rule: instagram | aspect | "4:5 to 1.91:1, 9:16".
Image rule: linkedin | aspect | "1.91:1, 1:1, 4:5".
Image rule: instagram | min-width | "1080".
Image rule: any | alt-text | "required".

## Design
Text on a Vamo design must be easy to read and must stay clear of the edges. Normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against the background behind it. Large text, meaning 24 px or more on a 1080 pixel wide post or 18.66 px or more in bold, needs at least 3:1. These are the WCAG 2.2 level AA values. Keep every text box at least 10% of the width away from every edge, which is 108 px on a 1080 pixel wide post. This matches the margin guide Canva shows on an Instagram post. It is Vamo's own rule, not a value that Instagram publishes. Wording on a design follows the same claim rules as the caption.

Design rule: instagram | contrast-normal | "4.5".
Design rule: instagram | contrast-large | "3".
Design rule: instagram | safe-margin | "108".

## Typography and layout
Vamo designs use the same two typefaces as the Vamo website: Fraunces for headlines and DM Sans for everything else. Use at most 2 font families in one design. On a 1080 pixel wide post, set the headline in Fraunces Regular (weight 400) at 72 to 120 px, in sentence case or lowercase. Set body text in DM Sans Regular (weight 400) at 28 px or more. Set labels and the vamo wordmark in DM Sans SemiBold (weight 600) at 24 to 36 px. The safe margin from the Design section applies to every text box.

Design rule: instagram | font-headline | "Fraunces 400, 72 to 120 px".
Design rule: instagram | font-body | "DM Sans 400, at least 28 px".
Design rule: instagram | font-label | "DM Sans 600, 24 to 36 px".
Design rule: instagram | font-families | "2".
