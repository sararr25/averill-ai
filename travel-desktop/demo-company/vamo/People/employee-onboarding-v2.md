# Vamo — Employee onboarding and learning

Version: 2. Owner: Lina Sørensen, People lead. Synthetic demo guide, 3 October 2026.
Intended status: approved after workspace review. Department: People.

The administrator adds the employee and department. A department lead reviews proposed sources before employees use them as company guidance. Personal notes remain private until the owner proposes them.

Each employee signs in with a separate local email/password account on this Mac. Sign out before changing accounts; restart requires sign-in. These accounts do not synchronize across devices. Employees use visible approved department sources. Company-wide guidance requires administrator approval; leads approve only their own department sources. Personnel files remain private to the administrator.

Never share account passwords as company knowledge. The administrator retrieves local login documents separately. Disable/reset and password change are local account controls; no invitation email is sent.

Employees explicitly choose work to share and can stop sharing. Canva lesson completion is employee-confirmed practice, not verified mastery. Weekly practice is formative learning; this demo defines no manager performance score or employment evaluation.

This guide establishes People context only. No specialized People automation is implemented.
