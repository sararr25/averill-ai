# Full Averill comparison: control A and task-oriented B

30 October 2026. Status: technical comparison complete; independent human study pending; **zero human participants, no human winner**.

## Scope and hypothesis

The owner reports that Averill and its Canva companion require too much scrolling and explanation, and ordinary questions receive unclear or misrouted answers. We predict that indexed questions, separated Ask/Checks/History views, explicit document versions and a dedicated Canva Guide entry will improve unassisted task completion.

A is the exact source working tree frozen when this request began, including the earlier retrieval and starting-action increments. B is the current implementation. Both keep the same permission, source, consent, Stop and employee-control boundaries. The control manifest records the copied source hashes; no normal accounts, keys or profile were copied.

This is a comparison of **whole product packages**. Several changes differ together. It cannot identify the individual causal effect of a label, typography or one navigation control. The technical harness uses identical synthetic inputs and does not estimate human time savings.

## Separate evidence layers

1. All automated contracts run on both source snapshots: accounts/roles, imports, sources, retrieval, checks, public statements, model validation, practice, bridges, cancellation and scope.
2. Identical scripted renderer/IPC, four-role accessibility, synthetic Canva design/extension/guide and offline rehearsal flows run on A and B.
3. Eighty fixed question/scenario prompts run offline on both. Exact citations, no unsolicited outbound calls and no false checked-state are tested; semantic answer quality remains separately reviewable. Natural-language descriptions of lifecycle problems do not simulate lifecycle events; the dedicated regression suites do.
4. Real provider requests use generated synthetic or generic public content with their existing preview/consent paths. They do not prove editor usability.
5. Installed real Canva, final packaged behavior, native VoiceOver and independent human task completion have distinct acceptance receipts. Synthetic tests never stand in for those receipts.

## Human feasibility study

Recruit at least five consenting people unfamiliar with Averill across employee, lead and administrator roles. Use anonymous participant codes. First conduct a small counterbalanced within-person pilot, with A-first/B-first randomly allocated and equivalent task sets to reduce carryover. Do not describe the pilot as a statistically powered A/B experiment.

Use the study recorder to capture **actual** outcomes. It starts empty and exports a local JSON file; no telemetry or messages are sent. Never enter credentials, real company content or contact details. Run both source versions on separate synthetic profiles through the study launcher. The installed extension must match the selected variant; the launcher does not install or switch browser extensions.

Primary pilot measure: fraction of prescribed tasks completed correctly without facilitator intervention within three minutes. Secondary measures: time, first click, wrong turns, assistance, source/measurement understanding and SEQ ease 1–7. Guardrails: no unintended edit/send/export/publication, no role/private-source disclosure, correct exact Undo, readable stale-state recovery and reliable Stop/logout. Stop the session immediately for a guardrail breach.

The facilitator gives outcomes only, never control names, navigation instructions or the owner test guide. If intervention is necessary, mark the task assisted rather than retroactively successful. Record unsupported behavior as a failure or limitation, not a completed task.

### Outcome-only task set

1. Reach your assigned synthetic workspace and identify what Averill can help with.
2. Find the approved process for requesting time off and open its evidence.
3. Find out how to put a photo into a shape in your design tool.
4. Get help improving readability, spacing and alignment; explain what was actually measured.
5. Improve a supplied draft without adding a new offer or fact.
6. Find a current document and identify its version; compare two specified documents.
7. Return to an answer you received earlier.
8. Find and correct a supplied forbidden claim, review the change and restore it.
9. Investigate a public statement and explain an inconclusive result.
10. Follow an appropriate Canva task guide and exit it without changing unrelated work.
11. Resume a practice activity and distinguish current measured progress from manual confirmation.
12. Stop sharing, then sign out; verify earlier content is no longer available to another synthetic person.

## Main experiment decision rule

There is no measured human baseline or eligible traffic count yet. After the pilot, pre-register the main experiment's allocation unit, baseline, minimum worthwhile effect, sample per arm, exclusion rules, full duration, confidence method and stopping rule **before** enrollment. Do not invent those inputs from technical harness results. For a whole-product test use parallel randomized arms to avoid carryover. Report individual task failures as well as aggregate success, confidence intervals and every guardrail outcome. Do not peek and declare a winner early.

Until actual consented participants and the registered sample exist, the human conclusion remains **inconclusive**. Vendor descriptions, automated testers, source hashes, screenshots and simulated roles are not human research participants.
