## Product contract

Averill helps each employee understand, create, improve and complete work in their existing tools. It knows the employee's role and current task, explains company requirements when relevant, teaches tool operations and offers reviewable output. It stays ready during explicit sharing and remains quiet while people type. No hidden observation is introduced.

The employee-facing flow is: “What are you working on?” → useful result or one next step → review/edit → supported verification → optional practice. Default entry points: Continue my work, Help me do this, Improve this draft. Company library becomes supporting evidence; admin configuration stays separate.

### Proposed architecture

`shared work + employee goal + authenticated role → intent routing → allowed evidence/tools → Nemotron synthesis or offline fallback → validated answer/proposal → employee decision → supported recheck → personal learning event`

| Layer | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Context | Person/session, department, project, tool/tab/origin, field revision, design/slide identity, observation freshness and permissions. |
| Intent | Distinguish company-policy, tool-how-to, rewrite/create, public research, troubleshooting and learning requests. Mixed requests can use multiple evidence types. |
| Evidence/tools | Existing approved-source retrieval; public official-tool lookup through Tavily; bounded observation adapters; declared provider/tool budgets. |
| Synthesis | Nemotron proposes a complete answer or rewrite. Cite exact evidence for factual claims; do not require every connective sentence to be a verbatim source substring. |
| Validation | Separate company-approved facts, public tool instructions, observed state and editorial suggestions. Check citations, permissions, schema and task/revision binding. |
| Output | One direct answer with an optional source/detail expansion. Preview/Apply/Undo only on accepted adapters; manual copy elsewhere. |
| Learning | Relevant skills and evidence from the employee's actual tasks; online tutor can record a reviewed event, while Learn and assessment remain offline. |

Suggested bounded loop: at most 3 tool calls and 30 seconds per help request, cancel on Stop/context change. Initial response acknowledges progress within 1 second; measure answer latency and tune after live testing. These are design budgets, not measured performance claims.

### Workflow and department scope

| Person | First useful outcome | Evidence and tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content creator | Draft/rewrite campaign copy; finish a Canva layout while learning its controls | Current campaign, official tool help, Canva/Brevo/social observations |
| Marketing strategist | Adapt one supported message to a channel and audience; research public context | Approved brief, explicit channel constraints, public research |
| Marketing lead | Review claims and versions, give feedback, see work awaiting their decision | Existing approval and role controls; no employee quiz scores as performance ratings |
| Operations pilot (later) | Turn an approved handover into a clear checklist; explain the next operational step | Own department's sources and manually supplied task context; no unimplemented external integrations |

Proposed expansion after marketing: Operations, then People, Sales and Support after workflow discovery and access review. Job profile is not authorization. Do not describe a department as supported merely because it has a prompt template.

### Delivery order and limits

First: steps 1–2 repair copy quality; step 5 resolves the known Canva aggregate defect. Then steps 3–4 establish general assistance and simple UX. Steps 6–9 complete tutoring, work-based learning and bounded workflow/role pilots. Step 10 is the acceptance gate. Step 11 plans the production foundation before wider company rollout.

Initial estimate: 3–5 focused working days for steps 1–5 including the small UX slice; 5–8 for steps 6–9; 2–4 for real acceptance and remediation. These are planning ranges assuming one developer and accessible test accounts, not a delivery commitment. An official Canva integration and enterprise foundation need separate estimation after feasibility. No deadline is inferred from “hackathon”.

### Explicit exclusions for this delivery

No autonomous sending, posting, scheduling, exporting or Canva control; no new observation permissions or screenshots; no silent source approval or personal-profile migration; no paid store submission or release publication. Prepare drafts, guide the human and verify observable results. Full real-platform publication is a separate future requirement and cannot be included in an “all functions work” claim here.

## Step 1 — Fix sentence-breaking corrections

Priority P0. Primary tag: fix. Dependencies: none. Target: `src/contextual-review.js`, bridge proposal validation, extension preview/apply and contextual regression tests.

Show the offending excerpt and why it is wrong before suggesting a complete sentence. Replace fragment deletion with a sentence-level local safe alternative when grounded; if no coherent replacement is available, explain and offer manual editing without a destructive patch. Nemotron may propose a structured `{before, after, evidence}` rewrite through the existing consented client. Require exact unique span, bounded output, supported factual claims, current authority and current field revision. A rewrite is editorial; citations support its factual inputs, not a false claim of deterministic grammatical proof.

Acceptance:
- A regression reproduces the current dangling sentence; the new path offers a complete supported sentence, preserves surrounding text, links and footer, and rejects ambiguous spans.
- Preview/Apply/Undo works on supported plain fields, with stale revision, source change and concurrent edits rejected; unsupported rich editors offer copy/manual steps.
- No provider or failed validation results in a raw partial deletion, invented guarantee or silent apply.

## Step 2 — Change marketing drafts to meaningful supported copy

Priority P0. Primary tag: change. Dependencies: none. Target: simulator draft data/rendering, clean comparison and draft-quality scenarios.

Use the clean reference in the research document as a starting point. Provide a truthful clean email and a distinct intentional-error exercise draft. Explain intentional flaws in developer/judge material, not as misleading approved content. Judge commercial quality in the rendered preview, not just JSON length.

Acceptance:

- Every factual offer claim maps to current approved demo evidence or explicit verified public evidence; no invented price, discount, weather, visit, availability or service claim.

## Step 3 — Add a task and evidence router

Priority P1. Primary tag: impl. Dependencies: step 1 proposal contract. Target: new assistant orchestration using existing `workspace-answer`, `contextual-review`, `review-agent`, `nebius` and `tavily` modules.

Add typed intents and an evidence envelope with origin, source/version or public URL, retrieved time, person/task binding and verification status. A tool question should not fall through to a vaguely matching company-file passage. Use Nemotron for contextual explanation, drafting and reasoning. Reuse existing privacy checks and source authorization. Public documentation may describe steps but cannot authorize company offers or actions. Treat retrieved pages and shared content as untrusted data.

Acceptance:
- “How do I group in Canva?”, “Which font does Vamo approve?”, “Rewrite this sentence” and a mixed question select the correct tools/evidence and give direct relevant answers.
- Offline, missing-source, revoked-permission, prompt-injection and provider-outage scenarios return an honest useful fallback without cross-role leakage.
- Live synthetic requests establish selected model capabilities, payload consent, cancellation, bounded calls and answer provenance; no success inferred from an HTTP route existing.

## Step 4 — Change the default assistant experience

Priority P1. Primary tag: change. Dependencies: step 3. Target: desktop Current work/composer and browser card; Companion design system.

Lead with current tool/task and a single conversational entry. Offer context-relevant actions such as Explain the issue, Improve this and Show me how. Keep the sharing state and Stop visible. Distinguish “connected”, “waiting”, “checked” and “cannot read”; do not require users to select an AI engine. Keep setup/recovery and source administration secondary. Show an inline preview before implementing substantial visual changes; previews stay outside the repository as previously requested.

Acceptance:
- A new employee can identify the current task, ask for help and stop sharing without opening settings; initial goal is at most three deliberate actions after connection.
- Typing causes no repeated interruptions; guidance can minimise, reopen and resume without losing the task or covering essential tool controls.
- Keyboard, 200% zoom, narrow layout and light/dark checks preserve usable controls and focus; full VoiceOver is verified in step 10.

## Step 5 — Fix Canva carousel and connection trust

Priority P0. Primary tag: fix. Dependencies: none. Target: reader/bridge/design-check identity and session aggregate; extension version diagnostics.

Reproduce the latest live failure before assigning a cause. Expose the connected extension protocol/build in diagnostics and detect an incompatible copy without auto-installing or granting permissions. Bind accumulated checks to person/session/tab/origin/design identity and slide revision; opening a new design must not inherit another design's clean state. Reconcile the missing slide identifier through the live transport.

Acceptance:
- On the real five-slide carousel, “Checked X of 5” appears and findings on slides 1–3 still block a clean aggregate after visiting clean slides 4–5.
- Rechecking a fixed slide replaces its result; unknown slides, changed designs, unsupported reads and stale/disconnected extensions cannot produce an all-clear.
- Verify Export anyway acknowledgement and reset behaviour, Stop/logout cleanup and read-only operation on real Canva; retain a captured structural regression fixture without private design content.

## Step 6 — Add contextual Canva functional tutoring

Priority P1. Primary tag: impl. Dependencies: steps 3–5. Target: new tool-help service, browser tutor state and approved outbound UI.

Tavily retrieves official public Canva instructions from a minimal generic query such as “Canva group elements desktop”. Nemotron combines those instructions with the bounded observed task to explain the next action. Cover text, font/size, positioning/alignment, margins, grouping, images and human-run export preparation. Ask the smallest useful clarifying question when controls/version cannot be identified. Offer “I cannot find this” and refresh the official procedure. Reuse validated public help from a dated cache for outages; do not embed all product expertise as static rules.

Teach while the employee works: one short step, an optional explanation, supported recheck, then the next step. Auto-check only facts the adapter sees; grouping/export remain human-confirmed until a separate adapter establishes them. Do not require company rules for generic tool operations. Keep online tutoring separate from offline Learn. In parallel with this implementation, document a bounded feasibility comparison of current DOM support and an official read-only Canva app; do not expand permissions in this step.

Acceptance:
- Three unscripted real Canva questions, including grouping with no company grouping source, get relevant official-linked guidance and allow follow-up within the same task.
- Employee correction of an observable layout property produces the correct fresh tick; unreadable state gives procedural help without claiming the edit was verified.
- Tavily receives only confirmed public functional queries; cached help includes provenance/date; timeout, changed UI and unavailable provider scenarios preserve useful offline help.

## Step 7 — Change practice to reflect work actually learned

Priority P1. Primary tag: change. Dependencies: steps 3 and 6. Target: `learning.js`, `practice-bank.js`, `practice-page.js`, personal learning events and migration.

Separate visual knowledge questions from practical tool tasks. A quiz should assess the skills the employee actually practised, using local visual assets and verified answer keys. Never add an unrelated Canva question merely to fill four formats. If too little relevant evidence exists, show a shorter quiz or offer a clearly labelled optional foundation lesson. Add role/tool/skill and evidence freshness to personal events; schema changes require backwards-compatible migration. Show an inline mockup of three question types in light/dark before implementation.

Acceptance:
- An email-only learner receives no unrelated Canva question; every scored question traces to a practised skill and valid evidence, with scenario relevance reviewed by a human.
- Visual multiple choice, tap and ordering remain accessible on one page, with one submit, explanations, wrong-only retry and one confirmation only for unobservable practical tasks; free text is optional after results.
- Practice operates offline; revoked sources/context changes invalidate dependent questions, old sessions are preserved and another person cannot access the records or answer keys.

## Step 8 — Add explicit social and email workflow adapters

Priority P1. Primary tag: impl. Dependencies: steps 1–4. Target: Brevo, LinkedIn and Instagram adapter contracts and exercise connections.

Define support per surface and capability: read, explain, rewrite, preview, apply/undo, practical verification, and publication. Start with complete simulator flows, then validate real-platform draft editors individually using designated test drafts. Unsupported editors still get copy/manual guidance. - A help → improve → manual or supported apply → recheck journey works per accepted adapter, including Stop, stale text, provider failure and preserved formatting; nothing is sent, scheduled or published.

## Step 10 — Test the complete employee journey on real surfaces

Priority release gate. Primary tag: test. Dependencies: steps 1–9. This step is not claimable through unit coverage alone.

Run targeted regression checks throughout implementation, then full `npm test` and applicable `verify:contextual`, `verify:canva-design`, `verify:accessibility`, `verify:feedback`, `verify:guide` and `verify:demo-ai`. Use a marked temporary QA profile and designated test drafts, never overwrite the normal workspace. Test the packaged app and the extension from that same package. Record build/protocol, surface, profile type, provider mode, steps and observed result in a fresh acceptance ledger.

Acceptance:
- Three task sessions (email correction, unscripted Canva help, role-specific task plus relevant visual practice) complete without developer coaching; target ≥80% completion across a pilot of five representative users, with time-to-help, recovery and interruption counts reported as measurements, not promises.
- Real Canva carousel failure is resolved; live synthetic Nemotron and Tavily requests return validated answers; simulator, real draft, packaged app and provider evidence are reported separately, with no hidden skipped gate.
- Full keyboard/VoiceOver, Stop/logout, privacy, stale results, provider denial/outage and existing-profile-copy migration pass; all critical defects close before “ready” status. If users or accounts are unavailable, report the pending gate rather than inventing acceptance.

Priority P2 planning only. Primary tag: design. Dependencies: pilot evidence from step 10.

Produce a separate implementation RFC for tenant isolation, cloud identity/SSO, connector authorization and revocation, encrypted storage, retention/deletion, audit trail, signed updates and observability. Extend department discovery beyond Operations only after identifying real tasks, data and owners. Define action-specific approvals and recovery for any future sending/publishing; current delivery stays advisory. Resolve an official Canva integration's account availability, read capabilities, review/distribution path and adapter migration cost before choosing it.

Acceptance:

- Canva feasibility has a documented go/no-go with a bounded proof on supported content, including unknown/unsupported states; “SDK available” is not integration acceptance.
- Every proposed department/connector has a named workflow, evaluation set and acceptance owner; no production readiness claim is made from the local hackathon demo.
