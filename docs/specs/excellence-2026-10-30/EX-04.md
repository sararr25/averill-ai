## Problem Statement

Substring matching and prefix truncation can miss relevant approved information and confuse languages or versions.

## Solution

Find the relevant passage across complete eligible documents with precise provenance.

## Implementation Decisions

- Filter visibility, approval, supersession, conflicts and confidentiality before retrieval.
- Index heading/paragraph chunks over complete eligible text with stable versioned source coordinates.
- Use lexical retrieval and optional semantic retrieval only under existing owner policy and consent.
- Unify assistant retrieval before generation; keep source facts distinct from rendered labels.

## Testing Decisions

- Ask realistic long-document and cross-language questions; verify returned evidence and outbound inputs, not index internals.
- Test observable behavior and employee-visible results, not private implementation structure. Prefer the existing authenticated renderer/IPC question → outbound preview → answer seam, following the Pro renderer and contextual bridge prior art. Preserve isolated synthetic accounts; company/public evidence, Stop/logout and content-bound consent are mandatory. The owner confirmed these testing seams on 30 October 2026.

## Out of Scope

Autonomous send, export or publication; arbitrary desktop observation or control; new screenshot/OCR permissions; automatic source approvals; cross-person memory; real employee data in Tavily; replacing human usability or real-platform acceptance with synthetic tests.
