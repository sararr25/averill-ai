## Problem Statement

Employees cannot reliably find the right action or obtain clear relevant answers without a test guide. Main Averill and its Canva companion present long content sequences, while tool help, company lookup, synthesis and public checking are conflated.

## Solution

Deliver the approved excellence plan as sixteen dependency-linked increments covering context correctness, answer evaluation, intent/retrieval, public checking, desktop/Canva navigation, learning, accessibility and real acceptance.

## Implementation Decisions

- Preserve the approved Companion identity, source authority, existing consent and bounded observation contracts.
- Implement dependency-linked EX-01 through EX-16.
- Keep existing parent and acceptance issues untouched.
- Treat the audit observations as hypotheses to reproduce where root causes remain unknown.

## Testing Decisions

- Test observable behavior and employee-visible results, not private implementation structure. Prefer the existing authenticated renderer/IPC question → outbound preview → answer seam, following the Pro renderer and contextual bridge prior art. Preserve isolated synthetic accounts; company/public evidence, Stop/logout and content-bound consent are mandatory. The owner confirmed these testing seams on 30 October 2026.
- Real providers, installed Canva/Brevo, VoiceOver and independent human usability remain separate gates.

## Out of Scope

Autonomous send, export or publication; arbitrary desktop observation or control; new screenshot/OCR permissions; automatic source approvals; cross-person memory; real employee data in Tavily; replacing human usability or real-platform acceptance with synthetic tests.
