## Problem Statement

Unit/provider success alone cannot establish that new employees can complete real work independently.

## Solution

Accept each layer separately and release only with honest evidence of the intended workflow.

## Implementation Decisions

- Require separate automated, live-provider, packaged installed-workflow and human acceptance receipts.
- Use at least five first-time participants across relevant roles with outcome-only tasks; report individual failures.
- Target at least 90 percent unassisted task completion and median ease at least 5/7 without claiming statistical certainty.
- Measure latency by path; do not infer percentiles from three samples.
- No automatic release or public publication is authorized by this acceptance specification.

## Testing Decisions

- Highest end-to-end packaged app/installed browser seam, with isolated synthetic accounts; live providers, VoiceOver and human study recorded separately.
- Test observable behavior and employee-visible results, not private implementation structure. Prefer the existing authenticated renderer/IPC question → outbound preview → answer seam, following the Pro renderer and contextual bridge prior art. Preserve isolated synthetic accounts; company/public evidence, Stop/logout and content-bound consent are mandatory. The owner confirmed these testing seams on 30 October 2026.

## Out of Scope

Autonomous send, export or publication; arbitrary desktop observation or control; new screenshot/OCR permissions; automatic source approvals; cross-person memory; real employee data in Tavily; replacing human usability or real-platform acceptance with synthetic tests.
