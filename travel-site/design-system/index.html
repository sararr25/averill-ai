04 / LAYOUT & EXPERIENCE

Space with a purpose.

Composition

  • 1320px maximum content width; fluid 20–72px side gutters.
  • Split hero and three destination columns on desktop. One column below 760px.
  • Spacing follows a 4px base; primary steps are 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 96px.
  • Rectangular photography, small radii for inputs and dialogs; pills for compact actions.
  • Real city photography, modest cropping and a caption. No invented location or testimonial.

Interaction

  • Filters update visible destinations and announce the count.
  • Native dialogs support keyboard navigation and Escape. Selection leads to the planner.
  • Every field has a label. Required destination validation happens before brief creation.
  • The generated brief is local: no reservation, payment or enquiry is submitted.
  • Visible focus, semantic headings, a skip link and reduced motion are part of the system.
