# Vamo — brand guidelines

Version 3.0 · 28 September 2026

Vamo is a fictional, youthful travel collective created as the demonstration company for Averill. Averill is the agent-assistant product presented at the hackathon. Their identities and interfaces remain independent. The existing Elseweek files inside the desktop demo are historical fixtures and require a separate coordinated migration.

## Position and voice

**Go somewhere good.** Travel for curious people who value places and the people they meet. Speak directly, warmly and with a little momentum. Focus on shared experiences and personal stories. Avoid luxury clichés, fake bookings, invented creator partnerships and fabricated engagement metrics.

## Identity

Warm cream `#FFF8E9`, sea ink `#0E576B`, mint `#BFE4D5`, coral `#F16D73` and small fuchsia `#D52C85` details. The large editorial type, candid travel photography and torn-paper touches make the site feel like a travel magazine with a social pulse. The Vamo mark is independent from Averill's aperture.

## Demo truth

The hero photo is generated concept imagery. Destination photos are credited real city photos. Creator-style tiles are editorial previews and not live social media content. The trip brief is built in the browser; no reservation, payment or enquiry is sent. The next phase may design Instagram and LinkedIn content, but this website does not claim those accounts are live.
